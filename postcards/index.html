iFlipbook Postcards

Otto’s Flipbook Diary · Volume One

Six places, and a cat who came along.

The ink dried. Otto and Momo went somewhere, stopped for a moment, and went on. Every frame of the two of them is hand-drawn in iFlipbook — the same frame-by-frame way you’d draw yours.

The places they walk through are landscapes from Coordigo, our other app, where you uncover a picture one square at a time. Drawing on a landscape isn’t in iFlipbook yet — these were put together in the studio. Consider them something to watch while we build it.
