Privacy Policy

Effective July 9, 2026

iFlipbook is made by Swiftian Inc. We keep things simple: the app works without an account, and we do not collect, sell, or share personal information for advertising.

Your drawings

Your flipbooks and drawings are created and stored on your device. If you enable iCloud, they may sync through your Apple ID using Apple’s iCloud, handled under Apple’s Privacy Policy. We don’t have our own servers that store your artwork.

Sharing & exporting

Exporting a video, GIF, or image, or sharing a project, happens only when you choose to. Content leaves your device only through the share options you pick.

What we don’t do

We don’t require personal details, and we don’t embed third-party advertising or cross-app tracking SDKs. Purchases, when offered, are handled by Apple through the App Store.

Children

iFlipbook is family-friendly and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will appear here with a new effective date.

Questions? Contact us.
